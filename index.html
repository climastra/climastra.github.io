About CLIMASTRA

Where earth-science research meets engineering practice

Environmental hazards rarely act in isolation. Cloudbursts trigger flash floods and slope failures; glacial lake expansion threatens downstream hydropower assets; rapid urban growth strains drainage and airsheds.

CLIMASTRA was founded by earth-science and geospatial specialists. Instead of generic advice, we use validated physical models, satellite observations and field data, and we deliver results that stand up to technical review.

More about our approach →

01

Grounded in physics and data

Our models are calibrated against observed rainfall, river flow and weather data and built on peer-reviewed methods. No black boxes.

02

Outputs you can use

GIS layers, cross-sections, early-warning thresholds and technical annexes, in the formats your engineers and reviewers expect.

03

Easy to bring into a team

A small specialist team that can join an engineering consortium or bid team and deliver a flood, landslide or climate chapter on a tender timeline.
