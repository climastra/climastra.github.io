Products & Technical Services

Science-based studies for infrastructure, cities and industry

Our areas of work are built around the engineering, regulatory and climate-reporting needs of Indian agencies, developers and companies.

Hydrology, Hydrodynamics & Flood Risk

Catchment-to-floodplain modeling for water infrastructure, cities and disaster planning.

Who it's for: Water Resources Departments, hydropower developers, Smart Cities, highway and rail contractors, DPR consultants

  • Hydrological modeling. Rainfall–runoff and snow- and glacier-melt hydrology under current and future climate.
  • Flood hazard mapping. 1D/2D hydrodynamic modeling and return-period maps of depth, velocity and hazard.
  • Dam-break & urban drainage studies. Breach analysis and inundation mapping for emergency planning; drainage and waterlogging studies for cities.

Tools and data: HEC-HMS, HEC-RAS 1D/2D, SPHY, SWAT, IMD gridded rainfall, ERA5

Related research: Upper Ganga river discharge under climate change

River Morphology & Sediment Studies

How rivers move, erode and silt up, and what that means for structures along them.

Who it's for: Irrigation and flood control departments, river conservation programmes, bridge and highway consultants, hydropower operators

  • Channel migration & bank erosion. Mapping from multi-decade satellite records to show where and how fast rivers shift.
  • Sediment transport & reservoir sedimentation. Sediment loads, deposition patterns and loss of storage capacity.
  • Morphological inputs for river structures. Evidence for river training works, embankments, bridges and intakes.

Tools and data: HEC-RAS sediment transport, DSAS, Landsat / Sentinel-2 archives, Google Earth Engine

Geohazards & Mountain Risk

Landslide, slope and glacial-lake hazards along Himalayan corridors and settlements.

Who it's for: SDMAs, BRO, NHIDCL, rail and tunnel contractors, hydropower developers, DPR consultants

  • Landslide susceptibility & hazard zonation. Combining terrain, geology, rainfall and seismic triggers.
  • Slope stability assessment. For corridors, cut slopes and reservoir rims, with ground-deformation monitoring from satellite radar.
  • GLOF hazard assessment. Glacial lake inventory and monitoring, breach scenarios and downstream flood routing.

Tools and data: QGIS, Python ML models, SINMAP / TRIGRS, Sentinel-1 InSAR, HEC-RAS 2D, r.avaflow

Climate & Air Quality

Climate and air-quality evidence for cities, states and national programmes.

Who it's for: Municipal corporations, state climate change cells, Pollution Control Boards, multilateral and development agencies

  • Climate risk & vulnerability assessments. City, district, state and national scale, using downscaled CMIP6 projections.
  • Heat & extreme-weather analysis. Evidence for heat action plans and adaptation planning.
  • Air-quality assessment & clean-air planning. Emission inventories, source contributions, forecasting and health impacts.

Tools and data: CMIP6 / NEX-GDDP, ETCCDI indices (xclim), ERA5, WRF-Chem, CHIMERE, TM5-FASST, Sentinel-5P, CAMS

Related research: black carbon in the Central Himalaya

Emissions & Net-Zero Pathways

Sector-wise greenhouse-gas baselines and reduction pathways.

Who it's for: Listed companies, industrial units, city and state governments, development agencies

  • GHG inventories. For organisations, cities and states (Scope 1, 2 and 3).
  • Sectoral baselines & scenarios. Power, transport, industry, agriculture and crop burning, and cities.
  • Net-zero pathway analysis. Reduction options for each sector, including the air-quality benefits of each emission cut.

Tools and data: GHG Protocol, IPCC 2006 Guidelines, GPC (cities), EDGAR / CAMS inventories, CEA grid emission factors, Python scenario models

Environmental & ESG Studies

Technical studies behind environmental assessments and corporate climate disclosures.

Who it's for: Environmental consultancies, listed companies, renewable energy developers, lenders

  • Specialist environmental studies. Baseline hydrology, air dispersion, land use and ecological sensitivity mapping.
  • Catchment Area Treatment planning. Watershed prioritisation and treatment planning.
  • Physical climate-risk scoring. Of assets and supply chains for BRSR, TCFD and ISSB reporting.

Tools and data: AERMOD, HEC-HMS, Google Earth Engine, CMIP6 hazard layers, Python
